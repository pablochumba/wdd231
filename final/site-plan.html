Get Better Site Plan

Get Better is a fictional learning experience platform designed to introduce visitors to practical growth opportunities in music, cooking, and future skill-based topics.

1. Site Name

Site Name: Get Better

This name communicates growth, progress, and improvement. It aligns with a platform where users discover meaningful learning experiences and develop new skills over time.

2. Site Purpose

The site will help visitors explore learning experiences in music and cooking through clear descriptions, practical guidance, and an approachable reservation form. It presents the project as a creative learning space rather than a commercial business.

3. Scenarios

  • How can I find a beginner-friendly way to start learning music?
  • What kind of cooking experience would help me improve my skills at home?
  • How can I contact the instructor or request more information about a learning experience?

4. Color Scheme

Forest Green

Used for headings, main buttons, and the main brand surface.

Golden Yellow

Used for highlights, accents, and interactive call-to-action elements.

Warm Cream

Used for page backgrounds and soft content containers.

Charcoal

Used for body text, borders, and supporting details.

5. Typography

Montserrat: used for headings, section titles, and prominent labels to create a strong and modern visual hierarchy.

Lato: used for paragraphs, lists, and general body text to maintain readability and a clear content structure.
